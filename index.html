What I build

  • Small surfaces, finished

    Each thing here is small enough to finish: a puzzle, a post, a widget. Finished means tested, accessible and deployed.

  • One shell, several bundles

    The shell owns routing, tokens and progress. Each game is a separate build, fetched only when you ask for it.

  • Boring where it counts

    Progress lives in this browser, in localStorage. No account, no backend, no cookie banner.
